# Local Food & Drinks

Singapore's local favourites, shaped by its melting pot of cultures and cuisines, have placed it on the world foodie map. From fragrant chicken rice to rich, creamy laksa, a wide array of flavours is available at hawker centres islandwide.

## Local Drinks
Quintessentially Singaporean beverages to try:

- **Kopi (Coffee)** — Singapore's flavoured caffeine fix, made from caramelised coffee beans roasted with sugar and margarine for a rich, dark flavour.
- **Sugar Cane Juice** — Made from peeled sugar cane, best served over ice with a slice of lemon.
- **Teh Tarik** — Classic frothy milk tea, "pulled" (tarik) between cups to create the froth.
- **Barley Water** — Cooked barley drink, subtly sweet, served hot or cold.
- **Bandung** — Pink rose-syrup beverage made with thickened milk and rose cordial.
- **Milo Dinosaur** — Malt-based chocolate drink topped with a heap of extra Milo powder.
- **Singapore Sling** — Singapore's national cocktail, created at the Long Bar at Raffles Hotel; pink hue from grenadine and cherry brandy, topped with pineapple and lime juice.
- **Chendol** — Shaved ice with white coconut strips, green jelly and palm sugar.

## Dining Notes
Singapore has both elevated restaurants serving fusion fare and casual hawker-centre eateries — a wide range of ways to dine like a local. A number of local hawkers and eateries have received the Michelin Bib Gourmand award for quality food at accessible prices.

Hawker centres and food courts referenced elsewhere in this knowledge base (e.g. Chinatown Complex Food Centre, 328 Katong Laksa) are good starting points for trying these dishes and drinks in context of a day's itinerary.
