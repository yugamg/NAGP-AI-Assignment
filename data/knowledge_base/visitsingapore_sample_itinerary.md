# Enjoy Singapore In 7 Days

With a week to spare, discover how east meets west, nature and urban beauty intertwine, and the past meets present, with this guide to Singapore's most iconic spots.

## Day 1: Urban Nature, Modern Marvels
**Morning/Afternoon — Marina Bay:** An iconic district in Singapore, guaranteed to bombard your senses, with a mix of nature, arts and shopping.
**Afternoon — Gardens by the Bay:** Spend the day marveling at the flora on display at the Flower Dome, and exploring the mist-filled landscape of the Cloud Forest. (Indoor/covered attraction — good option if it's raining.)
**Evening — Marina Bay Sands & The Shoppes:** Explore floors of luxury retailers, with the largest collection of coveted designer boutiques in Asia. (Indoor.)

## Day 2: Thrills and Spills at Sentosa
**Morning — Sentosa:** Sun, sand and surf await on your second day. Sentosa island, off the city's southern shore, is known for white beaches and family-friendly activities. (Outdoor — best in good weather.)
**Afternoon — Universal Studios Singapore:** Experience the magic of the movies at this theme park, with rides spread across six movie-themed districts from Hollywood to The Lost World. (Mix of indoor/outdoor.)
**Evening — Singapore Oceanarium:** Embark on an odyssey beneath the waves and find captivating species from the deep. (Indoor — good rain alternative.)

## Day 3: Culture and Heritage
**Morning — Civic District:** Singapore's rich colonial history and bright future intertwine here, where museums and colonial buildings sit alongside skyscrapers.
**Afternoon — National Museum of Singapore:** Explore the city's culture and heritage at this grand old museum, where history comes to life. (Indoor.)
**Evening — National Gallery Singapore:** Housed in buildings that were once the country's City Hall and Supreme Court, boasting over 8,000 works of art. (Indoor.)

## Day 4: Wildlife Awaits
**Morning — Mandai:** Head to Northern Singapore for majestic animals, nature-themed attractions and lush greenery.
**Bird Paradise:** Home to over 3,500 birds, immersed in naturalistic walk-through aviaries reflecting different habitats of the world. (Mostly covered/outdoor.)
**Evening — Singapore Zoo:** Experience wildlife from furry and adorable to ferocious and majestic. (Outdoor, largely covered walkways.)

## Day 5: Hip Meets Heritage
**Morning — Chinatown:** Take in the sights and sounds of Chinatown, and visit the Buddha Tooth Relic Temple, Masjid Jamae Mosque and Sri Mariamman Temple.
**Afternoon — Chinatown Complex Food Centre:** Taste various cultures, from sweet to savoury, local to international, at the largest hawker centre in Singapore. (Indoor/covered.)
**Evening — Tong Heng Traditional Cantonese Pastries:** Toothsome pastries including candied melon pastries, savoury buns and their signature egg tarts. (Indoor.)

## Day 6: A Mix of Everything — Nature, Shopping, Party
**Morning — Singapore Botanic Gardens:** Spend the morning amidst nature and tranquility at this UNESCO heritage site, filled with beautiful flora and fauna. (Outdoor — weather-dependent.)
**National Orchid Garden:** Nestled within the Botanic Gardens, home to the largest orchid display in the world with over 60,000 plants and orchids. (Outdoor, partially covered.)
**Afternoon — The Halia:** Brunch located amidst the one-hectare Ginger Garden, specialising in contemporary European cuisine. (Indoor/outdoor seating.)

## Day 7: Peranakan Delights and Rich Culture
**Morning — Katong-Joo Chiat:** Nestled in the East, with rainbow-hued heritage shophouses, hip cafes and modern boutiques — one of Singapore's most charming districts.
**Afternoon — 328 Katong Laksa:** A piping hot bowl of laksa, a spicy coconut milk-based noodle soup with prawns, cockles and bean sprouts. (Indoor.)
**Evening — Kim Choo Kueh Chang:** Traditional wares and unique souvenirs — a one-stop shop for discovering Peranakan culture. (Indoor.)
