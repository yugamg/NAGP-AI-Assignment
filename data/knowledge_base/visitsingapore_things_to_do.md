# Top Things To Do in Singapore

The best things to do in Singapore, curated for first timers and repeat visitors alike, across several visitor-interest categories.

## Unique Experiences
Go beyond the usual activities and discover something refreshingly different.

- **AltitudeX** — Defy gravity, soar indoors and feel the adrenaline of indoor skydiving at AltitudeX on Sentosa Island. (Indoor.)
- **Forest Bathing by Forest with You** — Embrace nature's healing touch, immerse in lush greenery and rejuvenate amidst the serenity of a forest. (Outdoor.)
- **Rainforest Wild Adventure** — Glide through the canopy or swing high into the sky with Adventure+ activities, spotting animals beneath, including Okapi and Diana Monkey. (Outdoor.)
- **Ubin Bisect Kayaking** — Glide through serene mangroves and encounter diverse wildlife at Pulau Ubin. (Outdoor.)
- **Bird Paradise** — Asia's largest bird park, home to more than 3,500 birds from over 400 species, across 10 immersive walk-through aviaries. (Mostly covered/outdoor.)
- **Kulnari Mystery Golf** — Solve intriguing puzzles and play mini golf in this immersive mystery golf course. (Indoor.)
- **Synthesis Bar** — Mixology bar with eclectic concoctions in chic, vibrant surroundings. (Indoor.)
- **Exploria** — One of Southeast Asia's largest indoor, nature-themed multimedia attractions with interactive RFID touchpoints. (Indoor.)
- **Naturalist Night Adventure** — Wildlife safari adventure spotting nocturnal creatures under the moonlit sky at Sentosa Island. (Outdoor, evening.)
- **Trifecta** — Snowboard simulator, wave rider, and skate bowl in one venue. (Indoor.)

## City in Nature
Singapore is a city with green escapes, scenic walks, and photo-worthy nature spots, connected by ecological corridors between nature reserves and parks.

- **Gardens by the Bay** — Fusion of urban architecture and lush greenery; home to the Flower Dome and Cloud Forest conservatories. (Indoor conservatories + outdoor gardens.)
- **Jewel Changi Airport** — Indoor waterfall (the HSBC Rain Vortex) amidst world-class shopping and dining. (Fully indoor.)
- **Mandai Wildlife Reserve** — Singapore's wildlife park precinct (Zoo, Bird Paradise, River Wonders, Night Safari, Rainforest Wild). (Mixed indoor/outdoor.)
- **Fort Canning Park** — Ancient relics and historical gardens amid the urban bustle; occasional concerts and theatre. (Outdoor.)
- **Lazarus Island / St John's Island** — Relaxing beaches and marine life, a ferry ride from the mainland. (Outdoor.)
- **Intertidal Trails at Changi** — Marine wonders and coastal serenity. (Outdoor.)
- **Pulau Ubin** — Cycling trails, kayaking, and rustic charm, step back in time. (Outdoor.)
- **Sungei Buloh Wetland Reserve** — Wildlife, mangroves and serene wetland landscapes. (Outdoor.)
- **Kranji Marshes** — Wetlands, diverse wildlife and tranquil trails. (Outdoor.)
- **Singapore Botanic Gardens** — UNESCO World Heritage Site, a blend of flora, fauna and history; includes the National Orchid Garden with over 60,000 plants. (Outdoor, partially covered.)
- **The Southern Ridges** — Scenic hike through treetop walks and city vistas, including the highest pedestrian bridge in Singapore (Henderson Waves). (Outdoor.)

## Culture & Heritage
Singapore was named Best Cultural Destination in the World (Tripadvisor's 2026 Travellers' Choice Awards).

- **Chinatown** — Chinese heritage through bustling streets, vibrant culture and cuisine.
- **Katong-Joo Chiat** — Peranakan heritage, charming streets and boutiques.
- **Peranakan Museum** — Traditional Peranakan art and artifacts. (Indoor.)
- **Hajjah Fatimah Mosque** — Mix of Islamic and European architectural styles.
- **Little India** — Indian cultural haven, heritage and excitement.
- **Singapore Sidecar Tour** — Guided vintage Vespa sidecar tour of cultural landmarks. (Outdoor.)
- **Kampong Gelam** — Rich history, living traditions, eateries and trendy shops.
- **New Bahru** — Heritage site (former Nan Chiau High School) now retail, dining and lifestyle.
- **The Istana** — Official residence of the President of Singapore; guided tours of its architecture and history. (Limited open days.)

For upcoming culture and heritage tours, talks and events, Catch.sg is Singapore's national platform for arts and culture listings.

## Iconic Architecture
Colonial buildings, bold modern designs, and world-famous landmarks that define Singapore's skyline.

- **CHIJMES** — Restored convent turned dining and entertainment complex.
- **Kranji War Memorial** — A solemn site honouring fallen soldiers.
- **Raffles Hotel** — Colonial-era luxury and heritage architecture.
- **Esplanade** — Performing arts centre known for its distinctive durian-inspired exterior. (Indoor venues.)
- **Marina Bay Sands** — Luxury resort with the iconic rooftop infinity pool, dining and entertainment.
- **Singapore Flyer** — Asia's largest observation wheel, panoramic views of Marina Bay. (Outdoor/covered cabins.)
- **Henderson Waves Bridge** — Highest pedestrian bridge in Singapore, wave-like design, panoramic views. (Outdoor.)
- **Old Hill Street Police Station** — Historic building known for its vibrant rainbow-hued facade, now government offices (exterior viewing).

## Family Fun
Kid-friendly activities and educational experiences for all ages.

- **Adventure Cove** — Water slides, snorkelling, and a lazy river. (Outdoor.)
- **Curiosity Cove** — Singapore's largest indoor nature-inspired playscape for kids 12 and under. (Indoor.)
- **Science Centre** — Hands-on exhibits, KidsSTOP, and an 8K 3D dome Omni-Theatre. (Indoor.)
- **Singapore Oceanarium** — Over 40,000 marine animals across 120 species. (Indoor.)
- **Wild Wild Wet** — Water park with slides and splash zones. (Outdoor.)
- **Bird Paradise** — Asia's largest bird park (see City in Nature above).
- **Disney Cruise Line** — Disney Adventure ship sailing from Singapore (multi-day cruise, not a single-day activity).
- **Sentosa Island** — Beach play, thrilling attractions and family time.
- **Singapore Zoo** — Rainforest zoo with naturalist, cageless habitats.
- **Bounce Singapore** — Trampoline park. (Indoor.)
- **River Wonders** — Asia's first river-themed wildlife park; giant pandas and Mekong catfish. (Mixed indoor/outdoor.)
- **Singapore Discovery Centre** — Interactive, multi-sensory exhibits. (Indoor.)
- **Universal Studios Singapore** — Theme park with six movie-themed zones. (Mixed indoor/outdoor.)

## After Dark
Singapore's nightlife, dazzling views, and after-hours experiences.

- **Big Bus Singapore Night City Tour** — Open-top double-decker night tour. (Outdoor.)
- **Night Safari** — World's first nocturnal wildlife park; tram or walking trails, 900+ nocturnal animals. (Outdoor, evening.)
- **Spectra – A Light & Water Show** — Nightly light-and-water show at Marina Bay Sands. (Outdoor.)
- **Eastern Coastal Loop Night Cycling** — Night cycling route along the east coast. (Outdoor.)
- **No.5 Emerald Hill Cocktail Bar** — Stylish bar with nostalgic charm. (Indoor.)
- **Zouk** — Singapore's iconic nightclub. (Indoor.)
- **Gx-5 Extreme Swing** — Adrenaline swing ride amid the city's night skyline. (Outdoor.)
- **Singapore River Cruise** — Night bumboat ride along the Singapore River. (Outdoor.)

## Museums & Galleries
Spaces filled with stories, history and discovery for all ages. (All indoor — reliable rainy-day options.)

- **ArtScience Museum** — Art and science exhibitions in a lotus-inspired building.
- **Children's Museum** — Immersive, interactive exhibits for young minds.
- **Mandai Gallery** — History and conservation story of Mandai Wildlife Reserve.
- **National Museum of Singapore** — The nation's history from colonial times to modern Singapore.
- **Asian Civilisations Museum** — Asia's diverse cultures, art and artifacts.
- **Hell's Museum** (Haw Par Villa) — Perspectives on death and the afterlife across religions and cultures, including the 10 Courts of Hell.
- **MINT Museum of Toys** — Asia's largest collection of vintage toys and collectibles.
- **Singapore Art Museum** — Contemporary Southeast Asian art.
- **Changi Chapel and Museum** — Wartime history, bravery and resilience.
- **Lee Kong Chian Natural History Museum** — Dinosaurs, fossils and biodiversity.
- **National Gallery Singapore** — World's largest public display of modern Southeast Asian art, in the restored former City Hall and Supreme Court.
- **Singapore Maritime Gallery** — Singapore's maritime heritage through simulations and exhibitions.

For upcoming museum tours, talks and events, Catch.sg is Singapore's national platform for arts and culture listings.
